References for the Power over Ethernet pages
============================================

The sources cited on the Power over Ethernet pages, grouped by kind. Each
citation in the text gives the section or table used. The standards
themselves, IEEE 802.3 :clause:`33`, :clause:`79` and :clause:`145` among
them, are listed on :doc:`../../standards`.

Summaries
---------

.. [POE-EA-BT] L. Yseboodt and D. Abramson, `Overview of 802.3bt, Power over
   Ethernet standard
   <https://ethernetalliance.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/WP_EA_Overview8023bt_V2p1_FINAL.pdf>`__,
   Ethernet Alliance white paper, 2019-12.

.. [POE-802-1AB] IEEE, `IEEE 802.1AB, Station and Media Access Control
   Connectivity Discovery <https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/802.1AB/6047/>`__,
   standard page (see also :std:`802.1AB`).
