Power input
===========

A board built around evampire can take its power from the Ethernet cable
(Power over Ethernet) or from a USB Type-C connector (USB Power Delivery).
Both methods need a short exchange of signals with the power source before
the source supplies full power. These pages describe the analog circuits
needed for that exchange.
