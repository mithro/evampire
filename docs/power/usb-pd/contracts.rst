Power contracts
===============

These pages explain how the two ends of a USB Type-C cable agree on more
power with USB Power Delivery messages.

:doc:`power-levels`
   The voltages and currents USB Power Delivery offers, and the messages
   that carry offers and requests.
:doc:`negotiation`
   The message exchange that sets up a power contract, and its time limits.
:doc:`source-sink`
   What the source and the sink each do to set up and keep a contract.
:doc:`cables`
   How a port powers and queries the chip inside a marked cable.
:doc:`role-changes`
   Swapping the power role, the data role and VCONN, fast role swap, and
   Hard Reset.
